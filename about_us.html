About Us

We are the official Game Development society of the University of Exeter, welcome to our webpage!
We are currently forming our own game studio, and are working as a team to develop our first project. If the idea of making a game sounds like fun to you, then we'd love for you to join us in creating our project! We welcome people of any experience level.

We also run many exciting events such as game jams, talks led by industry professionals, and workshops on game art, design, music, and storytelling.
